# Motion Video Production Brief: CogniFlow

## 1. Product & Business Purpose
- **Product Name**: CogniFlow
- **Category**: AI-powered Chrome Extension for Workflow Context & Task Continuity
- **Core Value Proposition**: Preserves workflow context, summarizes unfinished work, and eliminates mental rebuilding when returning from interruptions.
- **Hero Message**: “What if your browser could remember where your brain left off?”
- **Target Audience**: Modern knowledge workers, product designers, software engineers, project managers who constantly juggle 20+ tabs across Slack, Jira, Figma, Notion, and Gmail.
- **Call to Action (CTA)**: `cogniflow.ai · Add to Chrome`

## 2. Technical Specifications
- **Duration**: 35.0 seconds
- **Frame Rate**: 30 FPS / 60 FPS deterministic timeline
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9
- **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 (Full HD master)
- **Audio Mix**: Ambient electronic score (−16 LUFS integrated), synchronized VO narration, crisp localized UI sound effects (+4 dB in-band)

## 3. Brand & Visual Design System
- **Background**: Soft Cloud `#F7F7FF`
- **Primary / Brand Action**: Electric Violet `#7357FF`
- **Secondary / Connection Lines**: Digital Blue `#A9BFFF`
- **Typography & Dark Contrast**: Midnight Ink `#171B35`
- **AI Panels & Cards**: Lavender Mist `#E9DFFF` (with 80% opacity glassmorphism)
- **Workflow Restored State**: Flow Green `#20C997`
- **Card Styling**: 16px corner radii, 1px border `#E2E4F0`, soft multi-layer shadow `0 20px 45px -10px rgba(115, 87, 255, 0.12)`
- **Typography**: Inter / Manrope geometric sans, clean tracking (`-0.02em` on titles, `+0.04em` on uppercase badges)
